Generate a brand identity board as an image, not code. Strategy first: category, audience, function, emotional promise, what to avoid, and one core metaphor.

The logo comes from that metaphor (monogram + meaning, product action, metaphor fusion, negative space, or construction geometry): simple, scalable, ownable, usable as icon, wordmark, badge and pattern. No random animals, fake crests, generic bolts or sparkles.

Default output is one 3x3 board (4:3 or 16:10) with even gutters: logo cover, construction, digital application, brand essence, color system, typography, physical application, image direction, system detail. Other layouts (2x3, 2x2, 1x3) on request. Keep typography sparse, negative space large, colors few and muted with one strong accent, and the logo identical across every touchpoint. With references, match their rhythm, density and quality, never their logo, name or composition. Avoid startup gradients, neon, meaningless blobs, fake tiny UI and slide-deck templates.
