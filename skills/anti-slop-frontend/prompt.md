For landing pages, portfolios and marketing redesigns; not dashboards, data tables or native apps (say so and point elsewhere). Before any code, state one line: "Reading this as: <page kind> for <audience>, with a <vibe> language, leaning toward <design system or aesthetic>." Ask exactly one question only if the read genuinely diverges.

Set three dials from the brief (baseline variance 8, motion 6, density 4; minimalist/editorial about 5/3/2; trust-first or public-sector about 3/2/5) and let them gate layout, motion and density. Use a real design system (GOV.UK, USWDS, Carbon, a customized shadcn) when the brief calls for one. On a redesign, choose preserve or overhaul, audit brand tokens, IA and SEO first, and never silently change URLs, nav labels, form fields, the logo or legal copy.

Avoid AI tells: purple gradients, a centered hero over dark mesh, three equal cards, Inter + slate-900, John Doe/Acme/99.99%, "Elevate"/"Seamless", div-built fake screenshots, em-dashes in visible copy. Pre-flight: one theme and one accent, hero headline of at most 2 lines with the CTA above the fold, no repeated section layouts, `min-h-[100dvh]`, reduced motion, empty/loading/error states, WCAG AA contrast.
