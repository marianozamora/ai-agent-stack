Generate website design reference images, not code: one separate horizontal image per section (16:9, 16:10 or 21:9), never a tall full-page image or a collage. Commit out loud to the count ("Generating N images, one per section"); a landing page defaults to 6 sections and a full or marketing site to 8. Label each "Section X of N: <name>" until all are delivered.

Hold one brand world across every image: palette, type family and scale, CTA family, radius language, image treatment. Vary each section's composition anchor, background mode and scale (some giant, some nearly empty). Do not default to left-text/right-image heroes; keep hero copy minimal. Avoid AI slop: purple glow, meaningless blobs, dashboard card spam, fake KPIs, marquees, beige-serif "luxury".

Before finishing, check the hierarchy, generous and even spacing, a clear hook-proof-action path, and that the image count equals the section count; generate anything missing.
