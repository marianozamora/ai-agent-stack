Turn a decision the user can't answer alone into a Markdown questionnaire for the person who can. Interview the user only about the send, not the subject — they can always answer that:

1. Who receives it, and what do they know that the user doesn't?
2. What decisions or facts must the user get back?

Write questions aimed at that gap, most important first, one idea per question with an answer stub beneath it and a one-line "why this matters" only where the question could be misread. Frame the document as a discovery questionnaire: purpose, who it's from/to, brief context, how to answer (deadline, partial answers welcome), themed sections, a closing catch-all. Save it as `to-questionnaire-<slug>.md` and report the path.
